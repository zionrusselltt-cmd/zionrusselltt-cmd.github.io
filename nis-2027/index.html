Guide for employers · Updated 26 November 2026

NIS goes up to 19.2% on 4 January 2027.

From Monday 4 January 2027, the total National Insurance contribution in Trinidad and Tobago rises from 16.2% to 19.2% of insurable earnings. Here is what it means for your payroll.

19.2%The new total rate, up from 16.2%.
4 JanThe change starts on Monday 4 January 2027.
16Earnings classes. The classes and their earnings bands stay the same.

What changes.

The total contribution rate rises from 16.2% to 19.2%. The 16 earnings classes (I to XVI) and the earnings in each class do not change. Only the amount paid for each class goes up.

As before, the employer pays twice what the employee pays. Based on the official tables, that is about 12.8% from the employer and 6.4% from the employee (it was about 10.8% and 5.4% in 2026).

Example: the top class

Class XVI covers weekly earnings of TT$3,138 or more (monthly earnings of TT$13,600 or more). The weekly contribution for one employee changes like this:

One contribution for each Monday

NIS is counted by the week. For weekly, fortnightly or monthly pay, you pay one contribution for each Monday in the pay period. A month therefore has four or five contributions. January 2027 has four Mondays (4, 11, 18 and 25 January), all at the new rate.

When to pay

Contributions for a month are due by the last day of that month. NIBTT allows payment up to the 15th of the following month. After the 15th, NIBTT charges a 25% penalty, and interest after that. Hand in the NI 184 and NI 187 forms with your payment.

PAYE is separate: it is due to the Board of Inland Revenue by the 15th of the following month.

Your checklist.

  1. Update your rates

    Use the new contribution table for every Monday from 4 January 2027.

  2. Plan the cost

    Your employer contribution goes up. For one employee in the top class, it is TT$62.60 more each week.

  3. Tell your staff

    Take-home pay goes down slightly. In the top class, the employee pays TT$31.30 more each week.

  4. Pay on time

    Keep paying by the last day of the month, and never later than the 15th of the next month.

VIKI Books changes to the new rate by itself.

The 2027 table is already built in. From 4 January 2027, every pay run uses the new rate for each Monday, then fills in your NI 184 and NI 187 forms and the NIB upload file.
