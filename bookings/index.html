Questions

What hotel owners ask first.

Ask anything else on WhatsApp, or book a demo and see it with your own rooms.

How much does it cost?

TT$1,200 a month for the whole hotel or guest house. Any number of staff and devices, with rooms, restaurant, bar, recon, your own booking page, guest emails, backups, updates and WhatsApp support included.

Who sets it up?

We do. Your rooms, prices, menu and logo go in first, your staff practise on a practice hotel at their own pace, then you go live.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works on a computer, tablet or phone, with nothing to install.

Will I still get bookings from the big booking sites?

Yes. Their calendars are kept in step with VIKI, and guests can also book from your own booking page. A room can never be double-booked.

We are a small guest house with no restaurant. Is it still for us?

Yes. VIKI Bookings is made for hotels and guest houses of every size. The restaurant and bar till is there if you run one.

Is our information kept private?

Each hotel has its own copy of VIKI at its own address, kept apart from every other hotel. It is backed up every hour, and every person signs in with their own login.
