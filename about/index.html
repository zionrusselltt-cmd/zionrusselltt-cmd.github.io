About VIKI Systems

Practical software for the way businesses really work.

VIKI Systems is a technology company in Trinidad and Tobago. We build software that simplifies how businesses run: their money, their people, their customers and their guests.

Most business software is built somewhere else, for somewhere else. We start with the work in front of you: the books, the payroll, the VAT return, the front desk at check-in time. Then we make it simple.

Vision

Every business, starting here in Trinidad and Tobago, running with the clarity and control of a much larger company.

Mission

To take the slow, error-prone work out of running a business, with systems that already know the local rules and speak in plain words.

Philosophy

Software should fit the business, not the other way round. Every screen should explain itself. Every number should be one you can trust and check.

How we build

Four things we hold to.

01

Built around the real work

BIR forms, NIS rates, VAT periods, room tax and service charge are part of the system, not left for you to work out.

02

Plain words

Help on every screen and no training manual. Staff learn the restaurant and bar till in minutes.

03

Your data is yours

Download a backup or export everything to Excel whenever you like. You never have to ask us for it.

04

A person to talk to

When you need help, you reach a person here in Trinidad and Tobago on WhatsApp.
