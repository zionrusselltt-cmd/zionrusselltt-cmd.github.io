Questions

About pricing.

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need a card to try VIKI Books?

No. The free trial lasts 30 days and needs no card. Every feature is switched on while you try it.

Is every feature really included?

Yes. Each plan is one monthly price with every feature of that system included. The only extras are the add-ons above, if you want them.

I run more than one business. How does that work?

Each business has its own books and its own monthly price, and you see them all together. Accounting firms pay TT$79 a month for each client company.

Is there a free trial for VIKI Bookings?

We start with a short demo, then set it up with you: your rooms, prices, menu and logo. Your staff practise on a practice hotel before you go live.

Does VIKI file my taxes for me?

VIKI Books prepares your VAT, PAYE and NIS figures and lists every deadline. You, or your accountant, check them and file them with the BIR and NIBTT.

What happens to my data if I stop?

You can download a backup or export everything to one Excel file whenever you like, so your books always go with you.
