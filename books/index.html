Questions

Before you try it.

Still not sure? Message us on WhatsApp and ask anything.

Do I need a card to try VIKI Books?

No. The free trial lasts 30 days and needs no card. Every feature is switched on while you try it.

Does VIKI file my VAT, PAYE and NIS with the BIR?

VIKI Books prepares the figures and fills in your VAT return box by box, in the order of the BIR form, and lists every deadline. You, or your accountant, check them and file them with the BIR and NIBTT.

Can I move across from my old system or a spreadsheet?

Yes. Export your lists from the old system and import them in the order the page shows. "Check first" shows what would be added and skipped before anything changes. Balances and every unpaid invoice and bill come across, and you can compare the two trial balances to the cent before you switch.

What happens to the NIS rate change on 4 January 2027?

VIKI Books works out NIS, PAYE and health surcharge at the rates in force on each pay date. It switches to the new NIS rate on 4 January 2027 by itself.

I run more than one business. Do I pay twice?

Each business has its own books and its own monthly price, and you see them all together. Accounting firms pay TT$79 a month for each client company.

Can my staff use it without seeing everything?

Yes. Every person has their own sign-in. Data entry staff never see your bank, profit, reports or taxes.

What if I stop using VIKI?

You can download a backup or export everything to one Excel file whenever you like, so your books always go with you.
