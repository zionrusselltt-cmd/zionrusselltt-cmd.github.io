What it does, on the screens you will use.

New

Read receipts: photograph them, VIKI types them in

Take photos of a whole month of receipts and supplier bills at once. VIKI Books reads each one and fills in the supplier, date, receipt number, items, VAT and accounts. You check it and save. Nothing goes into the books until you do.

  • QuickBooks has no Trinidad and Tobago version. VIKI reads receipts here, in TT dollars, the T&T way
  • Reads dates day first, prices that include VAT, and zero-rated food and fuel
  • The same photo is never read twice, and a receipt already in the books is flagged
  • The photo is kept with the expense or bill

Included for businesses. For accounting firms, an add-on from TT$75 a month per client. Ask us about it.

Tick off the bank statement in one click

Import the statement and VIKI Books has already found the match for each line: the payment, the date, even when the bank is a day behind your books. You check them, then accept them all at once.

  • Lines it cannot match: find, create or ignore, one click each
  • Bank rules for the charges that repeat every month
  • Reconcile to a difference of zero, and undo if you need to

Quote, invoice, paid

Choose the customer and what you sold; the price and VAT fill in themselves. When they say yes, the quote becomes the invoice in one click, with your logo and their payment terms. Record the payment and it is in your bank.

  • Send by email or as a WhatsApp message
  • Credit notes, customer statements, payment reminders
  • Bills and expenses kept apart, because only one of them is a debt

Payroll with NIS, PAYE and health surcharge worked out

Start a pay run for the pay date and every person's deductions are calculated at the rates in force on that date. Add overtime or a bonus, check the draft, post it. Wages and the employer's NIS go into your books in one go.

  • Payslips ready to print or send
  • Monthly NIS, PAYE and TD4 figures ready to carry over
  • TD1 deductions, staff loans and union dues taken off by themselves

Your VAT return, worked out for you

When VAT is due, open the return. Every box is already filled in from your books, numbered the same as the BIR form, so you just copy the figures onto e-Tax. Mark it filed and paid, and it comes off your list.

  • The TT$600,000 registration check over the last 12 months
  • Corporation tax, business levy and green fund levy estimates
  • Clearance certificates with their expiry dates
New

Room tax and service charge for guest houses and restaurants

Switch on hotel accommodation tax and a service charge once. Tick which lines each one goes on, rooms yes, dinner no, and each shows as its own line on the bill. Room tax is kept apart as money owed to the BIR, never counted as sales.

  • A monthly report of room tax collected and service charge earned
  • Products remember which charges apply to them

For accountants: every client on one page

Each client company has its own books, bank and VAT. You see them all together, with what needs attention. Combined reports put companies side by side with a total, and you decide which of your staff sees which client.

  • Review and year-end checks, and requests to your clients
  • Data entry access for client staff who should not see the bank or profit

Moving from QuickBooks or a spreadsheet

Export your lists from the old system and import them here, in the order the page shows. VIKI Books reads a QuickBooks export as it comes out. "Check first" shows what would be added and skipped before anything changes.

  • Balances and every unpaid invoice and bill come across
  • Your old reports are kept as PDFs with the business
  • Compare the two trial balances to the cent before you switch
