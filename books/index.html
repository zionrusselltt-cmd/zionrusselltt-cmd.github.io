Questions.

Do I need a card to try it?

No. The free trial lasts 30 days with every feature, and needs no card.

Does VIKI file my VAT, PAYE and NIS?

VIKI Books prepares everything: your VAT return box by box, the NI 184 and NI 187 forms, the NIB online upload file, and the TD4s with the e-Tax file. You, or your accountant, check them and file them with the BIR and NIBTT.

Can I move across from my old system?

Yes. Import your lists, balances and unpaid invoices and bills, and compare the trial balances to the cent before you switch.

Can I take my data with me?

Yes. Download a backup or export everything to Excel whenever you like.
