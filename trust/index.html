Trust and security

Your data is yours. Here is how we keep it safe.

Plain words about backups, who can see what, and what happens to your information. Nothing on this page is a promise we cannot keep.

Backups

  • VIKI Books copies your books automatically every night and keeps each copy for 30 days. Each copy is checked after it is made.
  • VIKI Bookings backs up every hour, and keeps daily copies for 30 days.

Your data, your control

  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising, and we use your books only to run VIKI Books for you.
  • Download a backup of your books whenever you like, or export everything to one Excel file. You never have to ask us for it.
  • Ask VIKI, the AI assistant, is off unless it is switched on for your account. Nothing is sent to the AI provider when you do not use it. Details in the privacy policy.
  • Ask us to correct your account details, or to delete your account and books when you leave. Copies in the nightly backups disappear within 30 days.
  • Each hotel on VIKI Bookings has its own copy of the system at its own address, kept apart from every other hotel.

Who can see what

  • Every person has their own sign-in.
  • In VIKI Books, data entry staff never see your bank, profit, reports or taxes.
  • Accounting firms decide which of their staff see which client, and what their clients' staff can do.
  • In VIKI Bookings, the owner, manager, desk and bar each sign in, and every action is recorded.

A record of every change

  • The VIKI Books audit trail shows who changed what, when, and the value before and after. Nobody can edit or delete it.
  • Close a month and nothing dated in it can be changed by accident. Reopening it is recorded too.

Signing in

  • Ten wrong passwords and sign-in to VIKI Books is held for fifteen minutes, so guessing a password does not work.
  • A forgotten password is reset by email.
  • Sign in to VIKI Books at books.vikisystems.live.

Help from a person

  • Support is on WhatsApp at 1-868-787-7871, from a person here in Trinidad and Tobago.
  • Email us any time at info@vikisystems.live.
  • Help is written into every VIKI Bookings screen in plain words, and staff can practise on a practice hotel without touching real bookings.
To be clear: VIKI Books prepares your VAT, PAYE and NIS figures and your deadlines. You, or your accountant, check them and file them with the BIR and NIBTT.
